Transparent background. A refined asymmetric open shield outline that subtly forms an open book at its lower center, with three small connected circular nodes integrated naturally in the upper right of the shield, expressing protective learning and connected intelligence. Strong clear silhouette, economical precise strokes, flat vector-like graphic rendered as a high quality raster, deep navy #243440 as primary color and muted sage #82945c as accent, excellent readability at small website-header sizes. Center symbol and fill 80 percent of square canvas. No words, no letters, no phone number, no additional text, no emoji, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup, no stationery, no watermark, no government seal, no laurel wreath, no certification appearance. One symbol only, no variants grid. Professional, warm, grounded education brand.
